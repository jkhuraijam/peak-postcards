Log your hikes and adventures

Upload your GPS tracks, or simply log the date and the peaks you reached. Every hike, run, ride, ski day or paddle becomes a postcard ready to print: 3D terrain where it climbed, snow when it was winter, a clean 2D map where it was flat, with the elevation profile and the peaks you summited. Working on a peak list (the NH 48, New England 67, Adirondack 46, Colorado 14ers or the 50 state high points)? Watch it fill in, with a postcard for each peak and a collage of the whole list.

⬆ Upload your tracks belowor no GPS needed: just the day and the peaks
🔒 Your hikes stay on your device. Your files and your log stay in this browser; nothing is uploaded unless you choose to share a link. (Map imagery, terrain and peak names load from public map services, like any online map. Visits are counted anonymously, with no cookies.)
How do I get my tracks?
  • Strava (all at once): strava.com → Settings → My Account → Download or Delete Your Account → Request your archive. Strava emails a .zip within a few hours; drop the whole zip here. Big archives (hundreds of MB) work but take a minute.
  • Strava (one hike): open the activity on strava.com → ⋯ menu → Export GPX.
  • Garmin Connect: open the activity → ⚙ → Export to GPX (or Export Original for .fit).
  • AllTrails, Gaia, CalTopo, others: export or download the recording as GPX.
Summit = your track passed within 150 m of the summit. Peak list and heights from the AMC 4000 Footer Club list; summit positions from OpenStreetMap.

Makes a short link anyone can open. It stays active while people keep opening it, and is deleted after the time below without a visit. Photos have their location data removed.

Only share photos you took or have permission to share. Anyone viewing a link can report it, and reported links may be removed.

Your links
What's stored, and how to delete it A shared link stores the postcards' data and photos (location data removed) on our share service. Each link is deleted automatically 24 hours or 7 days after its last visit. Delete it sooner with Delete under "Your links" or its private delete link, or remove everything with "Delete all my data". No names, emails or IP addresses are kept. Lost access to this browser? Your links still disappear on their own; or open one and use Report to ask for removal.
