HIKES · RUNS · RIDES · SKI DAYS · THE NH 48

Your adventures, as postcards

Drop in your GPS tracks and every hike, run, ride, ski day or paddle becomes a postcard ready to print: 3D terrain where it climbed, snow when it was winter, a clean 2D map where it was flat, with the elevation profile and the peaks you summited. Working on the New Hampshire 48? We also find every 4,000-footer you stood on and the dates you were there, with a postcard for each peak and one card with all 48.

🔒 Your files never leave your computer. Everything runs in this browser tab; nothing is uploaded or stored. (Map imagery, terrain and peak names load from public map services, like any online map. Visits are counted anonymously, with no cookies.)
How do I get my tracks?
  • Strava (all at once): strava.com → Settings → My Account → Download or Delete Your Account → Request your archive. Strava emails a .zip within a few hours; drop the whole zip here. Big archives (hundreds of MB) work but take a minute.
  • Strava (one hike): open the activity on strava.com → ⋯ menu → Export GPX.
  • Garmin Connect: open the activity → ⚙ → Export to GPX (or Export Original for .fit).
  • AllTrails, Gaia, CalTopo, others: export or download the recording as GPX.
Summit = your track passed within 150 m of the summit. Peak list and heights from the AMC 4000 Footer Club list; summit positions from OpenStreetMap.
